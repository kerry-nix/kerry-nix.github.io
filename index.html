About me

Neuroscience Graduate Researcher at University of Pennsylvania

🧠

I am a neuroscience PhD candidate and clinical data scientist in Dr. Brian Litt's lab at the Center for Neuroengineering and Therapeutics, University of Pennsylvania Perelman School of Medicine. I use clinical data and neural signal processing to improve patient selection for epilepsy surgery. Previously, at Stanford University School of Medicine, I used transcranial magnetic stimulation and EEG to study new treatments for pediatric epilepsy.

My work on optimizing anti-seizure medication taper in the epilepsy monitoring unit is published in Neurology: Clinical Practice, and my work using EEG to lateralize language is published in the Journal of Clinical Neurophysiology.

I am seeking summer 2027 internships in clinical data science.
