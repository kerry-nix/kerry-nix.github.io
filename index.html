Kerry Nix

Neuroscience Graduate Researcher at University of Pennsylvania
TL;DR

I am a Neuroscience and clinical data scientist researcher at the Center for Neuroengineering and Therapeutics, University of Pennsylvania Perelman School of Medicine. I develop strategies for patient selection for epilepsy surgeries, using clinical data science and biological signal processing approaches, advised by epileptologist Dr. Brian Litt. Previously, I worked with transcranial magnetic stimulation and EEG, researching new treatments for pediatric epilepsy, at Stanford University School of Medicine.

My work applying data science approaches to improve patient safety during hospital stays has been published in Neurology: Clinical Practice, and prior work on using EEG to lateralize language was published in Journal of Clinical Neurophysiology.
